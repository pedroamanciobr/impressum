Painel canais

Painel canais is a private desktop tool used only by Pedro Amancio Alves Junior to manage his own YouTube channels (Moneta Atlas, Reverso Lab, Escritura em Contexto / Scripture & Context, Same Day, Elsewhere / Unterdessen, anderswo).

It reads the channels' own analytics and comments through the YouTube APIs, shows the production status of each video, and helps the channel owner answer comments. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
