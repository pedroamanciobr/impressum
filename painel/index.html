Painel canais

Painel canais is a private desktop tool used only by Pedro Amancio Alves Junior to manage his own YouTube channels (Moneta Atlas, Reverso Lab, Escritura em Contexto / Scripture & Context, Same Day, Elsewhere / Unterdessen, anderswo).

This app uses YouTube API Services. With the channel owner's authorization it:

  • uploads the owner's finished videos to his own channels, with title, description, tags, thumbnail and schedule chosen by him;
  • reads the status, statistics and analytics of those videos;
  • lists comments on those channels and posts replies that the owner approves.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
