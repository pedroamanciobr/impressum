Privacy policy – Painel canais

Painel canais is a private tool run on the personal computer of its only user, Pedro Amancio Alves Junior, to manage his own YouTube channels. Responsible: Pedro Amancio Alves Junior, Rio Claro – SP, Brazil, professionalcontactforchannels@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

  • Analytics and reports of the channels owned by the user (views, watch time, audience retention, impressions, subscribers), through the YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs.
  • Videos, metadata and public comments on those channels, through the YouTube Data API, to list comments and post replies approved by the channel owner.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show statistics and comments of the user's own channels to the user himself.
  • Data and access tokens are stored only on the user's computer. There is no server, no account system and no other user.
  • Comment text may be sent to an AI assistant (Anthropic Claude) only to draft a reply, which the channel owner reviews before anything is posted.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the one above.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted from the user's computer on request, and in any case when it is no longer needed. Questions: professionalcontactforchannels@gmail.com.

Português (resumo)

O Painel canais é uma ferramenta privada que roda no computador do seu único usuário, Pedro Amancio Alves Junior, para administrar os próprios canais do YouTube. Lê métricas, vídeos e comentários desses canais pelas APIs do YouTube, guarda tudo só nesse computador, não vende nem compartilha dados e só publica respostas aprovadas pelo dono do canal. O texto de um comentário pode ser enviado a um assistente de IA (Anthropic Claude) apenas para rascunhar a resposta. O acesso pode ser revogado em myaccount.google.com/permissions.
