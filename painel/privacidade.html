Privacy policy – Painel

Painel is a private tool run on the personal computer of its only user, Pedro Amancio Alves Junior, to manage his own YouTube channels. Responsible: Pedro Amancio Alves Junior, Rio Claro – SP, Brazil, professionalcontactforchannels@gmail.com. By using the app, the user agrees to this policy.

YouTube API Services

Painel uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data obtained from Google is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses, collects and stores

  • Authorization tokens for each channel the owner connects (Google OAuth), stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Channel and video data of the owner's own channels: titles, status (private, scheduled, public), publication times, view, like and comment counts (YouTube Data API).
  • Analytics and reports of the owner's own channels: views, watch time, audience retention, impressions, subscribers (YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs).
  • Comments posted on the owner's channels, to list them and post replies that the owner approves.
  • Uploads: the app sends the owner's own video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and schedules to his channels, only when he asks for it.

The app does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated statistics and the public comments YouTube shows to the channel owner. It does not access other people's channels.

How the data is used and shared

  • Data is used only to show the owner the status and performance of his own videos and comments, and to carry out the uploads and replies he requests.
  • There is no server, account system or other user. Data is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used for any other purpose.
  • The text of a comment may be sent to an AI assistant (Anthropic Claude) only to draft a reply, which the owner reviews before anything is posted.
  • The app shows no ads and serves no third-party content.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage on the device

All data and tokens are stored in local files on the owner's computer. The app uses no cookies. This page and the app's home page are static pages without cookies or analytics.

Retention and deletion

  • Analytics, reports and statistics are kept while the channel remains authorized; the app uses each authorization daily, so authorization is confirmed at least every 30 days. Other stored channel data is refreshed daily and deleted or refreshed within 30 days.
  • When a channel is disconnected in the app, its token is revoked with Google and all its stored data is deleted immediately (at most 7 days).
  • If access is revoked in the Google security settings, all data of that channel is deleted within 30 days.
  • Deletion can be requested at any time at professionalcontactforchannels@gmail.com and is done within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Contact

Questions or complaints about these privacy practices: professionalcontactforchannels@gmail.com.
