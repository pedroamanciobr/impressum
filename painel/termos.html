Use

  • The app acts only on YouTube channels whose owner has signed in and authorized it with Google OAuth.
  • Videos are uploaded, and comment replies are posted, only when the channel owner explicitly asks for it in the app. The app never changes the visibility of existing videos or edits a video after upload without the owner's instruction.
  • How data is accessed, stored and deleted is described in the privacy policy, which you accept by using the app.

Ending use

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google security settings (security.google.com/settings/security/permissions) or from within the app, which then deletes the stored data of that channel.
